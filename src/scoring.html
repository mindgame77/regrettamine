Score = sections 1–4 + bonus − regulatory penalty, capped at 0–100.

Principles

  • Every fund starts with full points and loses them only for verified evidence about its own behavior.
  • Rates, not counts: bad events are measured as a share of all companies backed.
  • Copycat names are excluded.
  • A suit the fund brought differs from one brought against it.
  • Coverage is shown separately. Missing data lowers coverage, not the score.

4 · Conflicts of interest 5

Partners on competing boards, backing a founder's direct competitor, in-house companies that compete with portfolio companies. −1 per documented conflict, scaled by partner count. Can't go below 0.

5 · Track record bonus +3

+1 per 5 years of investing with no significant pattern of harm.

6 · Regulatory penalty 0 to −50

EventPoints
