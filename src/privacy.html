Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • We store your account, watchlist, alert settings, report views and reviews. That's it.
  • No ads, and we never sell your data.
  • Reviews are anonymous by default, and verification proof is never shown.
  • You can export or delete your data any time.

Who we are

Regrettamine (regrettamine.com, currently served at mindgame77.github.io/regrettamine) helps founders check venture capital funds before taking their money. It is operated by Alex Malytskyy ("we", "us"), who is the controller of your personal data.

Questions or requests: __CONTACT_EMAIL__.

What we collect

DataWhat and why
AccountEmail address and a hashed password, or, if you use Google sign-in, the name, email address and profile picture Google shares with us. Used to create and secure your account.
WatchlistThe funds you save (up to 100). Used to show your list and send alerts.
Alert preferencesWhich alert types you want by email. Used to send only those emails.
Report viewsWhich fund reports you opened and when. Used to apply the free limit (2 reports, then 5 more with an account) and to show your history.
ReviewsYour ratings and comments about a fund, your role and stage when it invested, and any verification details you provide (work email, LinkedIn profile or cap table).
Payments (later)When paid plans launch, payments will be handled by a third-party payment processor. We will receive confirmation of your plan, not your full card number.
Technical dataIP address and browser details that our hosting providers log automatically for security and reliability.

Storage on your device

If you are not logged in, we keep a count of the fund reports you have opened in your browser's localStorage so the free limit works without an account. Logging in stores a session token in your browser so you stay signed in. Clearing your browser storage removes both.

We don't use advertising cookies or ad trackers.

How we use it

  • Provide the service: your account, watchlist, alerts, report history and reviews.
  • Enforce free view limits and, later, paid plans.
  • Keep the service secure and prevent abuse such as scraping or fake reviews.
  • Answer your messages and correction requests.

We don't sell your personal data, don't share it for advertising, and don't show ads.

Legal bases (EU/UK): performance of our contract with you (account features), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, publishing fund information), consent (alert emails, which you can switch off any time), and legal obligations.

Founder reviews

  • Reviews are anonymous by default. Others see only a label such as "Founder, Seed", unless you choose to show your name.
  • Every review is checked by us before it is published.
  • Verification details are used only to confirm that you took money from the fund. They are never shown publicly or to the fund.
  • We keep the link between your account and your review internally. We disclose it only if the law requires us to.

Information about people at funds

Fund reports include professional information about investors and fund staff (for example names, roles and court or regulatory records) taken from public sources such as court records, SEC filings and the press. We publish it because founders have a legitimate interest in knowing who they take money from. If you are named and believe something is wrong, you can ask us to correct it or object; see Corrections.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase: hosts our database and handles sign-in, in the United States.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • Google: if you choose Google sign-in. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.
  • An email delivery provider: to send sign-in and alert emails.
  • A payment processor: once paid plans launch.
  • Authorities: only when the law requires it.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and security measures. Because they are in the US, your data may be transferred outside your country; where required, we rely on standard contractual clauses or similar safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Account, watchlist, preferences and report history: while your account exists. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion, and from backups within 90 days.
  • Verification details: deleted once verification is done, at most 30 days after you submit them.
  • Published reviews: until you delete them or your account. Scores they already contributed to may keep using them in anonymised, aggregated form.
  • Hosting logs: as long as our providers keep them for security.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to or restrict how we use it. Email __CONTACT_EMAIL__. We reply within one month (45 days for California requests) and may need to confirm it's you.

EU/UK: you can also complain to your local data protection authority. California: you have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We don't sell or share personal information as the CCPA defines those terms.

Children

Regrettamine is not for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, passwords are stored hashed, and access to our database is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For significant changes we'll tell account holders by email or on the site before they take effect.
