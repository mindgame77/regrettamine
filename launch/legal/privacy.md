# Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and what you can do about it.
Effective October 25, 2026 · See also [Terms of Service](../terms/)

## 1. Who we are
Regretamin (regretamin.com) shows founders information about venture capital, private equity and hedge fund firms from public sources. It is operated by **Know Your Group INC.**, a Delaware corporation ("we", "us"), which is responsible for the personal data described here. Questions or requests: **malytskyyo@gmail.com**.

This policy covers two groups of people: **users** of the site (sections 2–7) and **people named on firm pages** (section 8).

## 2. What we collect from users
- **Account.** Your email address, or, if you sign in with Google, the name, email address and profile picture Google shares with us. Used to create and secure your account.
- **Saved firms.** The firms you save and the firms you have unlocked, so you can find them again under My firms.
- **Credits.** Your credit balance and a record of each credit used or added (which firm, when).
- **Firm requests.** If you search for a firm we don't have yet, the name you typed and, if you give it, your email so we can tell you when it's ready.
- **Submissions.** If you send us information about a firm, we collect what you send, any evidence (such as documents or emails, including proof of your relationship with the firm), your contact details, and the date. See section 7.
- **Correction requests.** Your message, contact details and any documents you send.
- **Payment records.** Stripe handles payments; we never see or store card numbers. We keep your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, your plan and its renewal date.
- **Abuse prevention.** When a free credit is used, or information is submitted, we store a one-way hash of your IP address (not the address itself).

## 3. Free searches and your device
Without an account you get 2 free credits on your device. To count them, your browser stores a random ID in its local storage (`regretamin.anon`), and our database keeps the count against that ID. It does not contain your name or email. A new account gets its own 4 credits. Device credits are not transferred. Signing in also stores a session token in your browser, and starting a purchase stores a short-lived note of the plan you picked. Clearing your browser storage removes all of these.

We don't use advertising cookies, ad trackers or analytics tools.

## 4. How we use user data
- To run the service: sign-in, credits, unlocking firms, saved firms and purchases.
- To send sign-in links and, if you asked, an email when a firm you requested is ready.
- To review, verify and publish submissions, and to handle correction requests and replies.
- To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
- To keep records we need to comply with the law and to defend legal claims.

We don't sell your personal data, don't share it for advertising, and don't show ads.

## 5. Services we use
- **Supabase:** our database and sign-in (accounts, credits, saved firms). Sign-in link emails are sent through Supabase.
- **Stripe:** payments, subscriptions and promo codes.
- **Google:** only if you choose Google sign-in. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.
- **Resend:** sends the email telling you a firm you requested is ready.
- **GitHub Pages:** hosts the website. Page code also loads from the jsDelivr content network.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms. Most are in the United States, so your data may be processed outside your country. Where required, we rely on the transfer safeguards these providers offer (such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework).

## 6. Legal requests
We may keep and disclose information when we must respond to valid legal process (such as a court order or subpoena), to protect our rights, or to defend legal claims. We share with authorities only when the law requires it.

## 7. Information you submit about firms
- **Your identity stays private.** We don't publish your name or contact details unless you agree. We may have to disclose them if a court or law requires it.
- **Verification.** We use your evidence to check the information. We publish a submission only after verifying it against an independent source (which we then cite), or as a clearly labeled, attributed unconfirmed report with a named public source.
- **Anonymity is not guaranteed.** Details you include can sometimes identify you, even if we never publish your name.
- **Subpoenas and court orders.** We disclose a contributor's identity only in response to a valid subpoena or court order in an active lawsuit. Where the law allows, we will try to tell you first (using the email you gave us) so you can object or get legal help. We may choose to oppose requests we believe are improper, but we can't promise we will or that we'll succeed.
- **Retention.** We keep submissions, evidence and our review notes for up to 6 years so we can show where information came from and defend legal claims, unless the law requires otherwise.

## 8. People named on firm pages
If you are a partner, executive, limited partner or other person connected with a firm, we may show limited information about you.

- **What we show:** your name, role, and firm affiliation, and what public records and published sources say about you, such as court and regulatory records, sanctions listings and press coverage. Unconfirmed reports are shown only with a named public source, attributed to it, and labeled "Not confirmed by official records."
- **Sources:** court dockets, SEC EDGAR and IAPD, OFAC and other government sanctions lists, company registries, published press, and verified user submissions. Each entry shows its source and the date we last checked it.
- **What we don't show:** home addresses, personal phone numbers or email, family details, ID numbers, or your nationality or ethnicity. The one exception is when nationality is part of an official listing, such as a sanctions designation. We never present nationality, on its own, as a sign of wrongdoing.
- **Sanctions matches:** a "possible name match" means only that a name looks like one on an official list. We keep it labeled that way unless the official listing confirms it.
- **Why:** to help founders understand who they take money from, a matter of public interest. Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, our legal basis is legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)), which we have balanced against your rights, together with applicable freedom-of-expression exemptions. Information about legal proceedings is shown only as it appears in official or published records.
- **Your choices:** you can ask to access, correct or delete information about you, object to its use, or add a reply, free, at malytskyyo@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. We correct or remove what is inaccurate and remove items shown to be false. For accurate information, we weigh your request against the public interest in accurate information about investors. **We never charge to change or remove information.**

## 9. How long we keep it
- Account data, saved firms and credit records: while your account exists.
- Payment records: longer where tax or accounting law requires.
- Submissions, correction requests, and the source copies and edit history behind each firm page: up to 6 years, to show where information came from and to defend legal claims.
- Hosting providers keep their own logs for their own periods.

## 10. Your rights
You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to stop using it, by emailing malytskyyo@gmail.com. We may need to confirm it's you, and we won't treat you differently for asking.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or other U.S. states), you may have more rights, including complaining to your local data protection authority. **California and other U.S. states:** we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, about users or people named on firm pages.

## 11. Children
Regretamin is not for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

## 12. Security
Data is encrypted in transit and access to our database is restricted. No system is perfectly secure.

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For significant changes we'll also tell people on the site.
